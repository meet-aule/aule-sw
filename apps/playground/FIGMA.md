## 2. How the code cites the Figma

- **HTML.** Every screen of `public/index.html` is one `<section class="screen" data-screen="…" data-surface="paper|ink">`, and the comment right before it names its frame and node ids, for example `<!-- Figma: P02 Tu correo · Móvil 160:1624 / Escritorio 160:1755 -->`. A screen the Figma does not draw says `web-only` and names the components it is built from. A state inside a screen has its comment at the element that changes (`P04 · Marcada` at the box, `P13 · De vuelta` at the greeting, `A05 · Silenciado` and `A15 · Silenciado` at the microphone, `A16 Hola, soy Aule 477:190, first run` at the session's title, `Pendiente · Sesión del alumno 260:654` at A05's bar); a state the Figma does not draw says `web-only state of …` there (A05's, for a session a reload left open and for the check; the talk button's coach and bar; the session's, when Aule does not arrive, and its stage).
- **Words.** The child's screens (`a02`, A04 and its details, A05 and its check, A16's words, the coach, the line under Aule's face and «Mostrar mi tarea») take their words from `public/first-steps.js`, in the student's language, Spanish and English, one short sentence an element (D33); the words of a session's ending and of Aule not arriving are `public/session-messages.js`'s.
- **CSS.** `public/style.css` names each Figma variable next to its token and each component (with its variants' ids) above its rules, for example `/* Figma: Opción/Grande 160:1374 (Normal 160:1375, Seleccionada 160:1379, Foco 160:1384, Error 160:1388) */`.
- **JS.** `public/steps.js` holds the table of screens (`SCREENS`): each one's Figma code, name, node ids, surface, progress step and way back. The router (`public/screens.js`) draws the progress header from it.
- **Tests.** `test/figma.test.mjs` checks that every section has its comment, that the sections and `SCREENS` are the same set with the same node ids and surfaces, that this file names every Vigente screen, component, token and icon, and that the stylesheet names each component and variable.

### 3.3 The flows

```
(signed out) c01 ─No, es mi primera vez→ p01 ─Con mi correo→ p02 → p03 ─(a new address)→ p03-name
(signed out, a browser that signed in before) p13 ─← Atrás→ c01
p01 ─Continuar con Google | Otras formas de entrar (GitHub, Facebook, Apple)→ signed in
signed in → p04 (no students) or home
(signed out, a new family) p02 (its address and its name) → p03 → signed in
p04 ─Autorizo→ p10-name (the name, a grade if they like) ─Empezar su primera sesión→ a02 ─Sí, soy yo→ a04 (a first run) → a04-details → a05 → session (A16's words, the coach)
p10-name ─Empezar más tarde (or, with no session left in the plan, Guardar)→ home «Listo. Aule ya conoce a {nombre}.»
a02 ─No soy yo→ editor ─Guardar→ a02 (the new name)        a02 ─Volver→ home
home ─Cuéntale a Aule sobre {nombre}→ p05 ─Empezar entrevista→ p06-grade → p06 → p07 → home «Gracias. Aule ya sabe más de {nombre}.»
p05 ─En otro momento→ home
c01 ─Sí, ya tengo cuenta→ p13 → (the same ways) → home
p03 ─No me llega el correo→ p14 ─¿Sigue sin llegar? Escríbenos→ p15 (mailto:admin@meetaule.com)
home ─a student→ a04 ─a tile→ a04-details ─Seguir→ a05 ─Empezar la sesión→ session ─Terminar→ session-end ─Volver→ home
a04 ─No lo sé→ a05 (no objective)        home ─Configurar→ editor        home ─Ver resúmenes→ editor, at «Últimas sesiones»
session ─time over | the platform closed it | connection cut→ session-end
home ─the student whose session a reload left open→ a05 «¿Seguimos donde nos quedamos?» ─Seguir con la sesión→ session (same room, same time)
a05 «¿Seguimos…?» ─Empezar otra sesión→ (the open one is closed) a04
a05 reached past a04, its session closed since ─Empezar la sesión→ a04
session-end «Se cortó la conexión.» ─Seguir con la sesión→ a05 «¿Seguimos…?»
session (no tutor after 15 s) ─Intentar otra vez→ (the attempt is closed) a05, the same purpose
session (no tutor yet) ─Terminar→ session-end «Aule no llegó a esta sesión, así que no cuenta…»
session ─Mostrar mi tarea→ (the camera on the stage, on this device only) ─Listo→ one picture, then off
session (the camera on) ─Cancelar | 30 s with nothing→ off, nothing sent
```

Each step forward is an entry in the browser's history: Back and «← Atrás» go to the step before. Signed in, the steps before signing in are not stepped back into; during a session Back does nothing, and after it the end screen takes the session's place, so Back does not step into the session or its lobby.

Layout: on a phone (under 768 px) a paper screen is the viewport's height with 24 px of padding, the header, a body that grows and the foot at the bottom edge; from 768 px it is a 560 px column centred both ways, the foot after the content. ## 7. Differences from the Figma

Each is what the code does today; the owner can change any of them.

| # | Difference | Why |
|---|---|---|
| D1 | One page; each screen is a section shown by `screens.js`. Steps forward are history entries; during a session Back does nothing. | The flows are step by step; a session started by mistake costs a family its day. |
| D2 | A web-only `p03-name` asks the parent's name when the platform answers `name_needed`, then verifies the same code again. | The platform needs the name for a new family; the Figma has no name step. |

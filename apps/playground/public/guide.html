A guide for families

Aule is an AI tutor that talks with your child by voice, helps with homework and practises maths with them without giving away the answer.

1How to sign in

  1. Open playground.meetaule.com/session in the browser of a phone, a tablet or a computer.
  2. Choose «Soy padre de familia» (I am a parent) and, the first time, «No, es mi primera vez» (it is my first time).
  3. Sign in with your Google account or with your email (we send you a six-digit code). No password needed.
  4. Give your consent, tell us a little about your child if you like, and write the name they like to be called.

When you come back, choose «Sí, ya tengo cuenta» (I already have an account) and sign in the same way.

2The free trial

7 days free from the first session, one session a day, no card needed. The trial starts with your child's first session, not when you create the account.

3What a session is like

  • With your account open, choose your child and what they want to do today: finish homework, review for a test or learn something new.
  • The browser asks to use the microphone and the camera. The camera is optional: it lets your child show their notebook to Aule.
  • Your child talks with Aule by voice. If «Mantén apretado para hablar» (hold to talk) appears, they hold that button while they speak. They can also type.
  • Aule shows exercises on the screen and gives hints, without giving away the answer.
  • The session ends when your child says goodbye or presses «Terminar» (end), or when the time is up.

What your child needs

  • A quiet place, with little noise.
  • A phone, a tablet or a computer with a microphone.
  • Headphones, if you like: they are optional.
  • Their notebook and a pencil, if they want to work on paper.

4The Aule plan: 3 sessions in any 7 days

S/59 a month

3 sessions in any 7 days for your family: each one comes back 7 days after it is used.

A session counts once the tutor has started it. If it ends in under two minutes, it doesn't count: a slip of the finger doesn't use one up. Sessions belong to the family: if you have more than one child, they share them.

5After the trial

When the trial ends, write to admin@meetaule.com and we'll turn your plan on. The plan can't be paid for in the app yet.

6Your privacy

Aule uses your child's voice, and the image of their notebook if they turn the camera on, to talk with them and check their exercises, and doesn't store the session's audio or video. We keep your name and email and, for each student, what you choose when you set them up, and you can delete your account with everything stored whenever you like: our privacy policy explains it.
